Preamble

Joseph Hope Foundation is a Non-governmental, non partisan organization. It is an Interdenominational body that cuts cross ethnic groups and tribe. Its overall objectives as the name implies, focuses on programs and activities aimed at affecting lives positivelywith Biblical/Kingdom truths.

Vision
Building People, and Improving the lives of less priviledge men, women and children in rural and urban communities through the, education, health, economic empowerment and social amenities.
Mission
To inspire breakthroughs in the way the world treats the hopeless and to achieve immediate and lasting change in their lives.

Aims & Objectives

  • To reach the unreached through the Gospel.
  • Provide Free Medical Care.
  • Empowering Youths on skill Acquisition
  • Help the less priviledge to start up small scale business.
  • Helping the children of the destitute with free education (scholarship).
  • Organizing Crusades to urban and rural areas.
  • Organizing seminars for people living with HIV.
  • Sport Development.

Foundation Details

Registered NGO operating across Nigeria with partnerships in education, healthcare and community development.

Ambition & Collaboration

We are demanding from ourselves and our colleagues, set high goals and are committed to improving the quality we do for the less priviledge.
